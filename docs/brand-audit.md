# Version 8 brand and restraint audit

Reviewed October 19, 2026, against the live [Linq homepage](https://linqapp.com/) and Erell’s supplied screenshot. This is a visual study, not a pixel clone or an endorsement.

## Reference observations

The official desktop site uses a #141414 charcoal canvas, white headings, gray supporting copy, #c4ff20 lime capsule buttons, and outlined secondary actions. Its rendered font pairing is FK Display for headings, NB International Pro for body copy and Geist Mono for controls. At 1440px, the sampled hero heading is 64px with a roughly 70px line height and medium weight.

This demo carries those color, type-scale, spacing and control choices into the Linq-facing screens. It uses locally bundled Geist Sans and Geist Mono under the Open Font License, rather than copying proprietary font files. The official Linq lockup was already present. The homepage’s illustrated city scene is not reproduced.

The previous [Hallmark](https://github.com/nutlope/hallmark) and [No AI Slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop) references were checked as design and writing references. No package was installed and no automated Hallmark score is claimed. The useful principles here are a structure appropriate to the task, specific copy, deliberate hierarchy, and fewer decorative defaults.

## Applied changes

- Remove “A Linq growth experiment,” “THE GROWTH EXPERIMENT,” and the persistent “Interactive simulation · Fictional travel data” stamp.
- Replace the tilted phone, floating email and decorative flight card with one straight Messages preview showing an actual sample attachment.
- Preserve the approved campaign paragraph and readable company-selection context. Keep the requested funnel as three plain steps.
- Remove repeated role labels while retaining the developer line, full customer handoff, AI assistant greeting and contact subtitle.
- Keep one primary action per stage; use consistent lime or Plot Twist plum actions and quieter secondary controls.
- Reduce the two event-triggered feature notes to a visual and a short phrase. Retain manual dismissal and automatic expiry.
- Keep the About dialog for simulation details, Accessibility for caption settings, and the final modal’s non-sending explanation.
- Remove duplicate restart and explanatory copy where the existing control or story already communicates it.

Plot Twist remains visibly distinct in the traveler handoff, voice and Messages scenes. Native phone elements retain system typography. Travel facts, narration, four text sends and the fictional route website remain unchanged.

## Verification standard

Review the rendered desktop and mobile pages, all four prepared sends, contact reveal, attachments, two feature notes, voice playback and cancellation, keyboard dialogs and final CTA. Automated tests verify story mechanics; they do not establish visual quality or comprehension by a fresh viewer.
